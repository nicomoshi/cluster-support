Cluster Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Overview

Cluster is a native client for Hermes Agent. Cluster does not operate a hosted chat service. The app connects to the Hermes gateway you configure.

Data You Provide

  • Connection details, such as your Hermes host, path, and API key.
  • Messages, images, and prompts that you send to your configured Hermes gateway.
  • Optional voice input or text-to-speech settings if you use voice features.

Where Data Is Stored

Connection credentials are stored on your device using the system keychain. App settings and non-sensitive connection details are stored locally on your device.

Network Services

When you use Cluster, messages are sent to the Hermes gateway you configure. If you enable text-to-speech, the text to be spoken may be sent to Fish Audio to synthesize audio. Speech recognition and system permission prompts are handled by Apple platform services.

Analytics and Tracking

Cluster does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Cluster may register your device token with your configured Hermes gateway so that gateway can send notifications related to your sessions. You can manage notification permission in system settings.

Data Deletion

You can remove local connection profiles and credentials inside the app or by deleting the app from your device. Data stored by your Hermes gateway is controlled by that Hermes installation.
